About the Department

Established in the year 2013, the Department of Information Technology is the youngest among the departments at Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar.

The department has grown into a centre for excellence, innovation and research with dedicated faculty and staff, highly motivated students, modern laboratories and an innovative teaching-learning environment.

The department offers undergraduate programmes in Information Technology and postgraduate programmes in Data Analytics. Ph.D. programmes are also offered.

Vision

To build a rich intellectual potential embedded with interdisciplinary knowledge, human and professional ethics to make students globally competitive in education, industry, research and development.

Mission

  • To provide globally relevant, interdisciplinary and multifaceted knowledge in Information Technology.
  • To provide knowledge, facilities and support for industrial internships and higher education.
  • To nourish creativity, innovation and entrepreneurial spirit for solving real-world problems.

Head of Department

Dr. Vijay Kumar

The Department of Information Technology welcomes students to join the thriving computer community and become visionaries and change-makers of the future.

The department provides learning opportunities in fundamental concepts as well as emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Blockchain, IoT, Big Data and Soft Computing.
