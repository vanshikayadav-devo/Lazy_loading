About the Department

Department Overview

The Department of Information Technology was established in 2013. It is one of the academic departments of NIT Jalandhar.

The department focuses on teaching, research, innovation and development in the field of Information Technology.

Vision

To build rich intellectual potential with interdisciplinary knowledge, human values and professional ethics to make students globally competitive.

Mission

  • To provide quality education in Information Technology.
  • To encourage innovation and creativity.
  • To develop professionals capable of solving real-world problems.
  • To promote research and entrepreneurship.
